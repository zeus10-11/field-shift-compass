# 7-Slide Pitch Deck Outline (fill into any template)

**Slide 1 — Hook (15 s)**
- Big photo: a farmer transplanting paddy.
- Title: *"Her grandfather sowed on the first of June. That date no longer works."*
- Subtitle: Field Shift Compass — a climate twin for every farm.

**Slide 2 — The problem (15 s)**
- Climate is shifting the farming calendar: sowing windows, dry spells, heat at flowering.
- Existing tools: dashboards farmers don't open, and "AI says so" answers they don't trust.
- Survey context: only 6% of farmers cite AI as a trusted decision source vs 56% for agronomists.
- Line: "The question isn't more data. It's trust + translation."

**Slide 3 — What we built (60 s of demo)**
- Screenshot trio: shift charts + risk calendar → climate twin map → advice card.
- One sentence each layer:
  1. **Shift Detector** — what changed here since 2000 (onset, dry spells, heat, rain bursts) + a month-by-month risk calendar.
  2. **Climate Twin** — which region's *today* equals your *2040*, with a path slider proving the match is stable.
  3. **Shift Advisor** — ranked actions with evidence + confidence, plus a back-test over 26 seasons.
- Bonus screen: WhatsApp broadcast preview (the delivery format).

**Slide 4 — Trust by design (15 s)**
- Rule-based logic, every card shows its data source.
- Confidence = trend strength × record length, shown openly (High / Medium / Low).
- Agronomist console: approve, edit, correct → recalibration.
- Farmer voice notes close the loop (ground truth).

**Slide 5 — NASA data powering it**
- NASA POWER (MERRA-2 reanalysis): daily rainfall, temperature, humidity → all trends.
- NEX-GDDP-CMIP6: future deltas for the 2040 twin projection.
- Architecture ready for: GPM IMERG, SMAP soil moisture, MODIS/HLS NDVI, ECOSTRESS, NISAR radar (cloud-proof monsoon monitoring).

**Slide 6 — Validation & honesty**
- Detected shifts reconciled against IMD crop-calendar/observed behaviour.
- Back-test: the sowing rule flags difficult seasons as an internal consistency check.
- Uncertainty shown, never hidden; prototype vs pilot clearly separated.

**Slide 7 — Impact & scale**
- Any region with NASA data + a crop calendar → a working compass.
- Pilot plan: 1 district agriculture office, WhatsApp/IVR delivery, Telugu/English voice.
- Team & acknowledgements.
