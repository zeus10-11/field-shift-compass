# 2-Minute Demo Video Script

| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 0:00–0:10 | Title card, farmer photo | "This is Ravi's family paddy field. For three generations they sowed in the first week of June. The rain no longer agrees." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Farmer app → My Field tab | "This is Field Shift Compass. It reads NASA satellite rainfall and temperature for Ravi's district — over 25 years." |
| 0:25–0:50 | What Changed tab, risk calendar + charts | "The sowing window has drifted. June carries a 35% chance of an 8-day dry spell; July brings heavy bursts on 73% of years; October is dry 92% of the time. No single number — a pattern." |
| 0:50–1:10 | Climate Twin tab, drag the Climate Path slider | "Here's the signature idea: we compare Ravi's *projected 2040* climate with the climate of 36 regions *today*. His 2040 already exists — Raipur, Chhattisgarh, an 87% match. And watch the slider: the twin holds all the way to 2040, so the advice is stable. Here's what farmers there grow successfully, right now." |
| 1:10–1:30 | What To Do tab, advice card | "So the advice is concrete: shift sowing, pick a short-duration or flood-tolerant variety, clear drains before the burst weeks. And every card shows its evidence and a confidence score." |
| 1:30–1:42 | Proof tab | "The trust test: our rule replayed over 26 years flags 8 seasons — the ones farmers remember as bad years. That's validation, not vibes." |
| 1:42–1:50 | Field Chat tab, play voice note | "Delivered the way farmers actually listen — a voice note in their language. Not sure? Send a voice note back." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Agronomist console | "A local agronomist reviews, corrects, approves — and those corrections recalibrate the rules for next season. Trust is a loop, not a promise." |
| 1:55–2:00 | Logo card | "Field Shift Compass. Built with NASA open data. Adapt, one season at a time." |

**Recording tips**
- 1280×720 min, 30 fps; record the live app, not slides.
- Show cursor deliberately; let charts render before talking.
- Keep the backup recording of the same run (submit both links).
- The demo video should not use SI-generated voice/visuals without visible watermark (see submission rules) — record your own voice.
